In 2024, 40% of adults in developing countries saved in a bank or mobile account, up 16 points since 2021. Saving is not only about putting money aside. It is about giving that money time to grow.

The idea: compound interest. Your savings earn interest, and then that interest earns interest too. Rs 1,000 saved at 6% a year grows to about Rs 3,207 in 20 years, without adding a single rupee.
